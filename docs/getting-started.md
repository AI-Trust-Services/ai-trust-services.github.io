# Getting Started

This guide walks through the AI Trust Platform from the perspective of the people who use it. It picks up where the [Introduction](/docs/) and [How it Works](/docs/how-it-works) pages leave off, and follows the platform as a connected journey — from the moment you log in, through registration and assessment, to ongoing compliance and audit.

The platform is organised around **roles**. What you see after logging in — your landing page, your tasks, and the actions available to you — is tailored to your responsibility in the governance lifecycle.

| Role | Where the journey starts |
|---|---|
| **Application Owner** | Register an AI-enabled application and provide its business context, then complete the EU AI Act assessment. |
| **AI Engineer** | Provide the technical and model information and validate changes that may affect risk or compliance. |
| **Compliance Officer** | Review submitted assessments, issue the binding risk classification, and oversee the compliance programme. |
| **Administrator** | Manage users, roles, and platform configuration. |

A single person can hold more than one role, and the platform adapts the available views and actions accordingly.

## Your landing page

After signing in, you arrive on a landing page personalised to your name, role, and data. It surfaces your most important actions and information immediately, so you can start work without hunting through menus.

### Application Owner / AI Engineer

Owners and engineers land on a home dashboard built around their day-to-day work:

- A personalised greeting.
- A **Register an AI System** action card — the starting point for bringing a new AI-enabled application into the platform.
- **Open Tasks** — the assessment questions and actions currently assigned to you, with a direct jump into each one.
- **My AI Systems** — the AI systems you own, each showing its current risk classification and status.

### Compliance Officer

Compliance Officers land on a purpose-built dashboard oriented around review and oversight:

- Quick actions to **register an AI system** or **view the full registry**.
- A **Recent AI Systems** panel showing recently added systems with their preliminary risk classification.
- A **Ready for Review** queue — systems where an owner has submitted an assessment and is waiting for the Compliance Officer's decision, sortable by submission date and planned rollout date.
- Access to EU AI Act reference materials.

## Finding your way around

The platform's navigation is role-aware: menu entries reflect what you are responsible for, rather than exposing every capability to everyone. A persistent **search bar** at the top lets you find and jump directly to AI systems, models, and other items registered on the platform, without stepping through the menu.

Because the platform is in active development, capabilities that are not yet part of the current release are grouped separately in the navigation, so it is always clear what is available and functional today.
