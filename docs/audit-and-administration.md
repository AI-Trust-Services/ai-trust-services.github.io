# Audit & Administration

Governance work has to be defensible and well-run. This page covers the capabilities that sit around the core journey: the **audit trail** that makes every decision traceable, and the scoped **administration** role that keeps the platform configured.

## Audit trail

The platform manages AI systems, governance activities, assessments, and compliance evidence — and the changes to all of them need to be traceable for compliance and audit purposes. The **audit trail** gives Auditors and Compliance Officers a reliable, reconstructable history of what changed, when, and by whom.

Governance actions are captured consistently as auditable events, including:

- System lifecycle events — when a system is **registered**, **reclassified**, or **deleted**.
- **Classification workflow transitions** — submissions, approvals, rejections, requests for more information, and any tier or role overrides made by the Compliance Officer.

Because classification confirmation is a human-in-the-loop decision, recording these transitions means no governance decision is left without a trace. Combined with the [audit-safe snapshot dossier](/docs/ai-assisted-assessment#an-audit-safe-record-of-every-run) captured for each assessment run, the platform can show not only *who decided what* but also *on what information*.

## Platform administration

The **Platform Administrator** keeps the platform running without reaching into application or compliance content. The administration experience is deliberately **scoped to a reduced set of permissions** — a focused menu that reflects exactly the capabilities an administrator needs, rather than broad access across the platform.

Administrators manage:

- **Users and roles** — who has access and in what capacity.
- **Platform configuration.**

## Related capabilities

Monitoring, alerting, and decision-trace analysis complete the operational side of governance — giving ongoing visibility into deployed AI systems, raising alerts when conditions are met, and letting teams inspect individual decision traces.
