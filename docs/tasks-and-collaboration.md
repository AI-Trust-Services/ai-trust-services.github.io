# Tasks & Collaboration

Governance is rarely a solo activity. An assessment may need input from several people — the Business Owner who knows the business context, the AI Engineer who understands the model, and the Compliance Officer who owns the decision. The platform turns this shared work into clear, assignable tasks so that everyone knows what needs their attention.

## Assigning work within an assessment

Individual questions within an assessment can be **assigned to contributors**. Contributors can be set up during [registration](/docs/register-and-classify#registering-a-system) or added as the assessment progresses. Assigning a question routes it to the right person rather than leaving the whole questionnaire resting on one owner.

Because submission is gated on all assigned questions being confirmed, assignment also acts as a lightweight coordination mechanism: the owner can see what is still outstanding before the assessment is handed over to the Compliance Officer.

## The Task View

Rather than making people dig through assessments to find their pending work, the platform provides a dedicated **Task View** — a single, navigation-accessible page listing every assessment question assigned to you.

The Task View presents your work as a table you can filter and sort:

- The **assigned question** and the **AI system** its assessment belongs to.
- The **owner** of that AI system.
- The **date** the task was assigned.
- The **assessment stage**, shown as a step indicator across the flow — *Draft assessment started* → *Assessment under review* → *Risk classification approved*.
- An **action** to jump straight into the specific question and start working on it.

Filters let you narrow the list by AI system or owner, with a reset to return to the full view. The Task View only shows tasks assigned to you, so it stays focused on your own workload.

## A view of your day

Alongside the dedicated Task View, your [landing page](/docs/getting-started#your-landing-page) gives a consolidated picture of your current work — your open tasks, the AI systems you own, and recent activity — so the first screen after login already answers "what needs my attention today?"
